---
title: Reporting a lost access card
description: Get your door and gate access reissued quickly
updated: 2026-09-05
---

Report a lost or stolen card immediately — an active card that isn't in your
possession is a building security risk, not just an inconvenience.

::: callout Report this the same day
A lost card stays active until it's deactivated in the system. The longer
it's unreported, the longer someone else could use it to enter the building.
:::

## What to do

::: steps
1. **Call or visit the IT Section**
   Extension 118, or the ground-floor office. Do this before doing anything
   else — deactivating the old card is the priority.

2. **Confirm your details**
   You'll be asked for your staff ID and department, so the correct access
   profile can be copied onto the replacement card.

3. **Collect your new card**
   Replacement cards are usually ready within one working day. You'll need
   your staff ID card to collect it.
:::
