---
title: Connecting to KCC-Staff Wi-Fi
description: For laptops and personal phones
updated: 2026-09-16
---

The `KCC-Staff` network is available throughout the building and covers most
of the car park. It's separate from the guest network, so use this one for
work devices.

::: steps
1. **Find the network**
   Open Wi-Fi settings on your device and select `KCC-Staff` from the list.

2. **Enter the shared passphrase**
   Ask your department head or the IT Section for the current passphrase —
   it's rotated every few months and isn't published in this guide.

3. **Accept the certificate prompt**
   The first time you connect, your device may ask you to trust a network
   certificate. This is expected — accept it to continue.
:::

## If you still can't connect

Move closer to a hallway access point — signal is weaker in the archive room
and the ground-floor storage area. If that doesn't help, restart your
device's Wi-Fi and try again before logging a ticket.
