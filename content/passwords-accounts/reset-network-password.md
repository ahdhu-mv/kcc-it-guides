Use this if you've forgotten your network password, or IT has asked you to
reset it after a lockout. This resets the password you use to log into your
office computer, email and the shared drive — they all use the same account.

::: callout Before you start
You'll need access to your registered mobile number, since the reset code is
sent by SMS. If your number has changed, contact the IT Section directly
instead of using this guide.
:::

## On your office computer

::: steps
1. **Go to the sign-in screen**
   On the Windows lock screen, click `Reset password` below the password
   field. If you don't see this option, restart the computer and try again.

2. **Enter your staff ID**
   Type your staff ID exactly as it appears on your ID card, e.g. `KCC-0142`.
   This is not the same as your email address.

3. **Verify with the SMS code**
   A 6-digit code is sent to your registered mobile number. Enter it within
   5 minutes — after that, you'll need to request a new one.

4. **Choose a new password**
   Minimum 10 characters, with at least one number and one symbol. Avoid
   reusing your last 3 passwords — the system will reject a repeat.
:::

## If the reset option doesn't appear

This usually means your account was set up before the self-service reset
system, or the account is currently locked rather than just needing a
password change. In either case, call the IT Section on extension 118, or
drop by the ground-floor office, and bring your staff ID card.
